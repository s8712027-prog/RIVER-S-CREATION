# ANN/A current-line handoff — non-exact continuation restoration v10

## Authority and correction

This file supersedes
`A_CURRENT_LINE_HANDOFF_LOCAL_ORDINAL_MOMENT_FAIL_V9.md` for continuation.
V4–V9 remain immutable historical records of the bounded experiments they
describe, but they do not redefine the active reader's learning criterion.

The active provisional local-capability reader was never reverted.  The
continuation error occurred only in isolated development evaluators: exact
equality of a cross-source abstraction, culminating in the ordinal-moment
`(C,F)` key, was again made a success condition after that requirement had
already been removed from the developmental reading path.

The correction is definitive:

- an exact evidence token identifies provenance and permits replay/challenge
  audit;
- it is not a learned cross-source identity;
- exact key/token equality across sources is not required for A to learn,
  retain a recurrence, or continue reading;
- same-source module-local recurrence may drive the local reader;
- a cross-source resemblance remains a non-driving transfer hypothesis unless
  separately supported, and it must never merge source evidence;
- no future Pass/Fail gate may require an exact key to recur across sources.

## Current active model

unchanged.  Its established behavior is:

- one local recurrence is retained while the reader advances adjacently;
- two independent local recurrences may generate a two-coordinate move;
- only two independent modality-local confirmations may generate a
  three-coordinate move;
- no scalar score, averaging, evidence merge, semantic inference, or
  relationship inference is introduced.

The earlier 215/215 regression remains the activation evidence.  No model file
was changed by this correction.

implemented source-local behavior, not discovery of another cross-source key.

used without its later cross-source-exact add-on.  Under equal bounded reads,
compare the active local-capability policy with the frozen fixed-current
baseline separately on each new source and in aggregate.  The primary
